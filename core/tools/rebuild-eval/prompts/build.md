# Build this product

You are a developer. The folder `brief/` describes a product: its business intents
(`brief/intents/`) and, when present, its product model — a domain, its capabilities and their
functionalities, each page with what it does, its rules and its acceptance criteria.

Build the product as a working web app:

- `app/index.html` — one file, with its CSS and JavaScript inside. It must open in a browser as
  a file, with no server and no network.
- Sample data inside the app, so every screen has realistic content to show.
- `app/NOTES.md` — every place where the brief was silent or unclear and what you chose, one
  line each.

Build everything the brief describes, and nothing it does not describe. Where the brief is
silent, take the simplest reading and write it in `NOTES.md`. Use only what is in this folder;
do not look for anything outside it. Design and code are yours to choose.

When you are done, your last message is only: `built: app/index.html`.
