Thin: proposed from the person's words; deepen with `/discover-capabilities`.

## Intent
- Goal: Every gate a play declares names its kind, its way past and what using the way past records; the person who meets the gate sees its way past there, and using it lets the work go on and writes one owed entry with who used it, when, what was skipped and where it is kept; and today's gates that stop a person with no way past are listed, each with what its way past would record or why it keeps a person's yes, for the ADR on ways past a gate.
- Limit: A way past is shown at the gate and never hidden, and each use of it is recorded.
- Limit: The ways past that are built, such as a plan session's override and the thin ICE, keep working as they do now.
- Limit: The play checker's rules, the gate settings and the ADR itself stay with Standards, Settings and Manage ADRs.
- Fails if: A gate a play declares names no way past, and it is not on the list with why it keeps a person's yes.
- Fails if: A person used a way past and no owed entry names who used it, when and what was skipped.
- Fails if: A gate that stops a person today with no way past is missing from the list.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: each play's checkpoints and stops with their declared kind; the gate rule; the ways past that are built and what each records; the approved pages that name a way past or a stop; and the principle that every gate has a way past, with its ADR once it is written.
- Never read: any memory file by its path, and any ICE page.
- Persona: the developer or the product owner, who meets a gate while running a play and wants to see how to go on.
- Scope: In: the declaration of each gate's kind, way past and record; showing the way past at the gate; the owed entry on each use; and the list of today's gates with none. Out: the play checker's rules, adding a gate to a play, the gate settings, the ADR itself, where what is owed is kept, and the pinned yeses.

## Expectation
- Scenario: In a bank's payments service, a play needs a tracker type that is missing and not mapped. The gate's declaration names its kind, its way past and what using the way past records; the developer sees the way past at the gate, uses it, and the work goes on, and the issue's folder holds one owed entry naming the missing type, who went on and when. In the same week, the list of today's gates for the ADR on ways past a gate names a plan session's stop on uncommitted work, with what its way past would record or why it keeps a person's yes.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who built it reads every gate today's plays declare and finds each naming its kind, its way past and what using it records, or on the list with why it keeps a person's yes; uses one way past and finds its owed entry; and finds no gate of the stock-take missing from the list.
