## Intent
- Goal: A domain, capability or feature found while other work goes on gets its page, its ICE page and their index entries, written through the keeper and the model's skills, all or nothing, thin when it is thin with both marks in agreement, and linked to its intents on both sides, in the change already open and with no discovery run, so the model keeps up with the work.
- Limit: The model is written only through the page skill and the index skill, never by hand, and only inside the model.
- Limit: The page comes before its entry; an entry is never written for a page that is not there.
- Limit: An approved page is written word for word, and a thin page holds only what the person's words say.
- Fails if: A found part has a page with no index entry, or an entry with no page, or its two thin marks disagree.
- Fails if: A request with a bad part writes anything, or a file outside the model changes.
- Fails if: A found part lands in a second change, or a discovery play runs to add it.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the words that name the found part, its parent and its intents, the model's index, the page skill's modes and the index skill's request shape, the thin-mark rule, the ICE page's three parts, and the design of the shared step in #979.
- Never read: any memory file by its path, any ICE page, and any file in a plan's evals folder.
- Persona: the developer, a person or an AI agent who builds the code, who finds a part during work; and the product owner, who approves the words of a page.
- Scope: In: the found part's page, its ICE page, their index entries, the thin marks, its intent link on both sides, and the shared step. Out: placing a feature in a slice and under its epic, deepening a thin part, changing a part that exists, and the link rules.

## Expectation
- Scenario: In a clinic's booking app, the developer builds Booking and finds a reminder the day before is needed. In the open change, a thin Send a reminder feature is written under Booking with its thin ICE page, both entries are added to the index with the two thin marks in agreement, and the intent "patients book online" lists it on its Touches list. In a game studio's build, a request names a parent capability that is not in the index; nothing is written, and the error names the part.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who added it reads the change, the index and the skills' reports, and finds for the clinic the page, the ICE page, both entries, the two thin marks in agreement, the Touches line, every changed file listed and no second change; and for the game studio no file changed and the error naming the part.
