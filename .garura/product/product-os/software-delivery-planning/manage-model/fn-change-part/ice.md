## Intent
- Goal: A part that exists changes only from words or a place a person approved, through the keeper and the model's skills: named lines of any page, a whole approved page, an intent page from its draft, and a part's one-line, intents, issue, thin mark, capability or folder in the index, each all or nothing and keeping every other line and field; and each play that owns a record takes a person's edit the same way.
- Limit: The meaning of a change comes from the person or an approved design, never from the skill.
- Limit: An intent page reads confirmed only when the caller names the person who confirmed it, and a rewrite keeps its "What was done" block.
- Limit: A feature moves to another capability only on a person's approval, and a move that would break a link is refused.
- Fails if: A line the request did not name changes, or a field the request did not name changes.
- Fails if: A request with a line that is not on the page writes anything.
- Fails if: An intent page reads confirmed with no person named, or a move leaves a page that names the old path.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the person's approved words and where they are recorded, the page as it is now, the page skill's named-lines and approved modes, the intent-page skill and its draft, the index skill's update keys, the edit standard and the --edit work planned for each play that owns a record, and the rule that a move breaks no link.
- Never read: any memory file by its path, any ICE page, and any file in a plan's evals folder.
- Persona: the product owner, who approves the new words; and the technical lead, who moves a part to another capability or folder.
- Scope: In: named lines of any page, whole approved pages, intent pages from their drafts, the update keys of a part's entry, and the edit standard. Out: updates because what was built changed, repointing and taking out, a new intent's page, and a slice's record.

## Expectation
- Scenario: In a shop's checkout service, the product owner rewords two lines of the checkout intent; those two lines change, the rest of the page is kept word for word, and the page still reads proposed because no person was named as confirming it. In a bank's payments service, the technical lead approves filing Upload documents under Applications; its page and ICE page move, its entries follow, and a request line that was not on a page in the same week is refused with nothing written.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who made the change compares each page and entry before and after, and finds only the named lines and fields changed, the shop's intent page still proposed with its "What was done" block as it was, the bank's feature in its new folder with no page naming the old path, and the refused request with no file changed.
