## Intent
- Goal: Each checkpoint a builder writes into a play sits where the play's workflow shape puts it and declares its class, and pinned when the play's intent asks for it; a checkpoint is pinned or unpinned only through the play's ICE and /edit-play; and, once Give every gate a way past defines it, each gate the builder writes names its way past and what using it records, so every gate in a play is there on purpose and none stops a person in silence.
- Limit: No setting, policy or ledger pins or unpins a checkpoint; only a change to the play's intent does.
- Limit: A checkpoint is turned off only in the change that adds the checks that replace it.
- Limit: The way past and what it records follow Give every gate a way past; this feature writes them into the play and does not define them.
- Fails if: A compiled play's checkpoint does not declare its class, or a checkpoint the play's intent asks for is not declared pinned.
- Fails if: A checkpoint is pinned or unpinned by a setting or by a direct edit to the compiled play.
- Fails if: Once ways past are defined, a builder writes a gate that names no way past and no record.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the play's ICE and its workflow shape, the gate rule with its kinds and classes, the checkpoints compiled plays declare today, the gate settings, the definition of a gate's way past from Give every gate a way past, and the principle that every gate has a key, with its ADR once it is accepted.
- Never read: any memory file by its path, and any ICE page.
- Persona: the technical lead or the developer, who adds a checkpoint to a play; and the product owner, who decides when a checkpoint is pinned.
- Scope: In: placing a checkpoint by the workflow shape, declaring its class and pin, pinning and unpinning through the play's intent, and writing each gate's way past once it is defined. Out: defining the way past and what it records, the list of pinned yeses, the gate settings and the learned policy, the play checker's rules, and the rest of changing a play.

## Expectation
- Scenario: In a bank's harness repository, the technical lead builds a play that moves money between test accounts; its checkpoint sits before the move and is declared one-way-door and pinned, as the play's intent asks. A team member sets every checkpoint off in the settings, and the pinned checkpoint still waits for the product owner. In a shop's harness, the product owner unpins a review; the change is made in the play's ICE through /edit-play, and the play is compiled again.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who built the plays reads the bank's compiled play and finds its checkpoint before the move, declaring its class and pinned; turns every checkpoint off in the settings and finds the pinned one still asked; and, for the shop, finds the unpin in the play's ICE with a new fingerprint, and no pin or unpin in the settings.
