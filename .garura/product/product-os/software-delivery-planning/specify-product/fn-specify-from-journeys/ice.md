## Intent
- Goal: Each journey through a prototype is walked and recorded, the way its people would use it.
- Limit: It never changes data on a deployed site unless the person said that's safe.
- Fails if: A view it could reach isn't kept, or one it couldn't reach isn't recorded with why.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the prototype, the person's write-up.
- Persona: the product owner.
- Scope: In: working out who the prototype serves, walking each journey a step at a time, and keeping a picture and the text of every step. Out: keeping the prototype's views before the walk, and drafting intents from the journeys.

## Expectation
- Scenario: On a deployed site the person hasn't cleared, a step that clicks "Delete" is refused, and the step's record says why.
- Done means: Every captured view sits on a journey or is named as not walked.
