## Intent
- Goal: What a person gives becomes the right business intents, at the depth the product owner chooses, each confirmed by them.
- Limit: One need is one intent, and it can hold several features. Transitional parts aren't intents.
- Limit: It writes business intents and their sources only.
- Fails if: An intent is confirmed without the person's typed word, or a part that matters wasn't considered.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: what was given, the existing intents, the depth preference.
- Persona: the product owner, who confirms or drops each intent.
- Scope: In: drafting one business intent for each need, and saving each confirmed intent with its Sources. Out: sorting against existing intents, checking that what was given was fully used, keeping each kind of source, and writing domains, capabilities and features.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A prototype with three main sections gets an intent for each section, and each intent names the journey it came from.
- Done means: A person sees every part considered, each as an intent or with the reason it isn't one.
