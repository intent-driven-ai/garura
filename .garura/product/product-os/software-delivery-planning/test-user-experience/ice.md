## Intent
- Goal: Once the work is built and runs in the testing environment, tools run by a part that is not its builder, and a person who is not its builder walking the slice at each screen size its surface names, test it on each declared surface against that surface's properties and guidelines and the screen design in force, so that every part has a pass or a fail with how and where it was checked and the verdict is a pass only when every part passes; each finding goes back as a handoff plan to the developer when the builder can fix it alone, or to the product owner when a person must decide; and the tests run again as the product changes, when a surface is updated and when the screen design is updated.
- Limit: What is tested, and at which screen sizes, comes only from each surface's declared properties and guidelines and the screen design in force, read and never set; a tool checks only what needs no judgment or what a guideline says a tool checks; nothing that no tool checked and no person walked counts as a pass, and a tool's clean run never passes a part a person must judge.
- Limit: The builder never gets the tests, their results, the pictures, the walks or the verdict, and never walks its own work; only the handoff plan reaches it, and it names what a person meets, where and the way back, never the test, its rule, its id, its tool or a figure.
- Limit: Nothing is tested outside the testing environment, in production or on a builder's own machine; the records of the review of each change and of the build's separate test run are taken as evidence and not run again; and nothing comes from what the AI agent knows by itself.
- Fails if: The verdict is a pass while a property or a guideline of the surface, or a screen of the screen design at a size the surface names, failed, was not tested, or was a part a person must judge that no person walked.
- Fails if: A test or a walk touched production, a builder's own machine or a system the testing environment does not hold, or the builder walked its own work.
- Fails if: The builder was given a test, its results, a picture, a walk's record or the verdict, or a finding that needs a person's judgment or authority, such as a change of the design system or of the screen design, went to the developer.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A shop's checkout is built. Its browser surface says one layout reflows across a phone, a tablet and a desktop screen size and every screen works within the shop's design system, and the screen design shows each checkout screen as a wireframe. In the testing environment the tools open each checkout screen at the three sizes and keep a picture of each; every screen opens. The person the product owner named walks the checkout at each size beside the wireframes: at the phone size the pay button sits past the right edge of the screen, and the delivery choice is built from a list that is not the design system's, though the design system has one. The product owner sees a verdict with those two parts failed, each with the screen, the size and how it was checked. The developer gets two handoff plans: a shopper on a phone must scroll sideways to reach the pay button, so the page must fit the phone's width; and the delivery choice must use the design system's own list. No test, tool, rule or picture is in them. After the fix, the tools run and the person walks again, and both pass.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who ran the tools and the one who walked runs the tools again and walks the same steps at the same sizes on the same built work, gets the same verdict, and finds every property, guideline and screen with a pass, a fail or a reason it was not tested, each walk naming who walked, where and at which sizes, no part a person must judge counted as passed by a tool, no test outside the testing environment, and each finding sent where its way back says.
