Thin: proposed from the person's words; deepen with `/discover-capabilities`.

## Intent
- Goal: When a review opens, the person reads in one calm page the whole model's approval state first, then each business intent the run changed with its ICE under it, and the product as domain, capability and feature, then its limits and decisions, each piece in plain words with what changed and why, switching between the final words and the changes and between what changed and everything, on a laptop or a phone, so they can judge what was made without reading a file.
- Limit: No file path, field name or raw file is shown, and the plays and the plan are never shown.
- Limit: An ICE is answered only in Intents; Product shows it to read only, with a link.
- Limit: Reading writes nothing.
- Fails if: The overview does not show the whole model's approved, changed and in-progress counts each time it opens.
- Fails if: A piece the run changed is missing from the view, or shows without what changed or why.
- Fails if: The person has to open a file to know what a piece says.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the review pack, the whole model's approval state, the answers so far, and the page templates' section headings.
- Never read: any memory file by its path, any ICE page, and production.
- Persona: the product owner, who wants to know what the product now says without reading files.
- Scope: In: the overview, the Intents and Product sections, the two switches, each piece's view, and laptop and phone widths. Out: answering, the ADRs, Memory and Agent decisions sections, building the pack, and a dark page.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A school's timetable app's run changed one intent's proof and added a feature. The review opens on the whole model's counts, then a "Needs you" list with the intent first. In Changes, the old proof is struck through and the new words marked; under Product, the new feature sits under its capability, with why it was added. The ICE of the intent shows in Intents, and in Product to read only, with a link.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who built the page opens a review of a real run on a laptop and on a phone, and finds the whole model's counts first, every changed piece with what changed and why, each ICE answerable in one place only, and no file shown.
