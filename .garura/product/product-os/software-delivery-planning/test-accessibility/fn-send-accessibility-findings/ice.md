## Intent
- Goal: Once the tools have run and the walk is done, one accessibility verdict for the built work gives each part of the profile's pass bar, each capability's accessibility need and each surface's accessibility guideline a pass or a fail with how and where it was checked, names each part not tested or not walked with its reason and each guideline below the profile's level, is a pass only when every part passes, and says so plainly when no accessibility level is set; and each finding goes back as a handoff plan with what a person cannot do, where, in which way and the way back: to the developer through Build the work test first when the builder can fix it alone, to the product owner when a person must decide, and to Fix defects test first when the work was already accepted.
- Limit: The verdict takes the records of the review of each change and of the build's separate test run as evidence without running them again, and it is kept where no part that builds the work reads it.
- Limit: A handoff plan never names the test, its rule, its id or its tool, never gives a figure measured, and its way back is guidance, never code.
- Limit: A finding goes to the developer only when its way back is a change of the code the builder controls; one that changes the design system, the screen design, a part the builder does not control, a surface's declaration or guideline, the level itself, one not walked because no person was named, or one in doubt goes to the product owner, and only an approved decision moves the accessibility level.
- Fails if: The verdict is a pass while a part failed, was not tested or was not walked, or a missing accessibility level is read as a pass.
- Fails if: A handoff plan names the test, its rule, its id or its tool, or gives a figure measured, or the builder was given the verdict or a walk's record.
- Fails if: A finding that needs a person's judgment or authority, such as the colours of the design system, went to the developer, or a finding was let through with neither a fix nor an approved decision.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the results of the tools and of the walk with how and where each part was checked and who walked, each part not tested or not walked, the product profile's accessibility level and pass bar, each capability's accessibility need, each surface's accessibility guideline, the decisions that moved them, the records of the review of each change and of the build's separate test run, the approved screen design, the declared surfaces with their design system, whether the work was already accepted, and the recovery rule.
- Never read: the value of any secret, such as a password or a key; anything in production, such as its data or its traffic; and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who reads the verdict and decides each finding a person must decide; the developer gets back, as a handoff plan, each finding the builder can fix alone.
- Scope: In: the one verdict against the profile's accessibility level and each surface's accessibility guideline, and writing and sending each handoff plan. Out: running the tools and walking the build, fixing, updating a guideline or the screen design, setting or moving the accessibility level, recording a defect in the tracker, and running the tests again.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A clinic's booking page is tested. The verdict fails two parts and names a third, a check the guideline says a person makes with a screen reader, as not walked, because no person was named for this run. The developer gets a handoff plan: a patient using a keyboard alone cannot leave the dialog that confirms a visit; the keyboard must be able to close it. The product owner gets a second finding, that text on the clinic's brand colour is too faint on every screen, whose way back is a change of the design system's colours, and the part not walked, each with how and where it was checked. No handoff plan names a test, a tool, a rule or a figure.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who gave the verdict reads it beside the results, the walk's record and the pass bar, and finds every part with a pass, a fail or a reason it was not tested or not walked, each with how and where it was checked, no pass while a part failed or was not tested, each handoff plan with what a person cannot do, where and the way back, sent where the recovery rule's test says, and no test, tool, rule or figure named in any of them.
