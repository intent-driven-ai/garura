## Intent
- Goal: Once a web analytics or product analytics tool is connected, its reports on how people reach and use the product are read, on their own as the project's settings say, and each figure is kept as the tool gave it, with the tool's own name for it, the time it covers, the connection and when it was read, and each connection not read is named with the reason, so that what consumers want can be understood from what they really did.
- Limit: It reads only through the connected tool and only what its connection names; it never reads production or the product's own stores of data, and never writes to the tool.
- Limit: Only figures about many people are kept, never anything that names or points to one person; no figure is reworded, summed, compared with an intent, made up or carried over.
- Limit: When the reads run is the project's setting, and no read waits for a person's yes.
- Fails if: A figure kept differs from what the tool gave, or has no connection, time or date of reading.
- Fails if: A read that failed was kept as figures, or as nothing, without being named with its reason.
- Fails if: Something that names or points to one person was kept, or the tool shows a change made by a read.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the connected analytics tools, what part of each is read and where its key is kept, the project's setting for when the reads run, the product profile's privacy level and compliance needs, and the records of earlier reads.
- Never read: the value of any key, except at the moment a read takes it from where it is kept, and it is never written down; production and the product's own stores of data; and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who wants what consumers want understood from what they really did in the product.
- Scope: In: reading each connected analytics tool and keeping its figures as given, with where and when, and naming each failed read. Out: connecting the tools, reading what people say, understanding the figures, measuring an intent, and changing the product.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A shop's checkout has a product analytics tool connected for its checkout pages. The weekly read takes in, for each day, how many people opened the cart and how many paid, each figure with the tool's name for it, the day, the connection and when it was read. The tool's answer also lists the steps of one shopper by an account number; that list is not kept. The next week the tool refuses the key, and the record names the connection as not read, with the reason, and holds no figures for that week.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who set up the read compares what was kept with what the tool shows for the same days, and finds every figure equal, with its connection, day and date of reading, nothing that names or points to one shopper, the failed week named with its reason, and no change in the tool.
