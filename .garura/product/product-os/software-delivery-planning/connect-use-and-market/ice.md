## Intent
- Goal: The project connects, with the product owner's yes for each, to the tools and forums that hold how its product is used and what people say about it and its market, web analytics, product analytics, user feedback and forums such as Reddit; records each connection in its settings by name, with where its key is kept; takes in what each holds as the tool gave it, with the connection, where it was and when it was read; and keeps the connections current as the product and its tools change, so that what consumers want and what the competition is doing can be understood from what really happened.
- Limit: It only reads, and only through the connected tools: it never writes to a tool or forum, never reads production, and never changes, sorts or interprets what it takes in, nor any intent, the model or memory.
- Limit: No key's value and nothing that names or points to one person goes into the settings, a record, a log or what is taken in; the settings name only where each key is kept.
- Limit: Each connection, and each change or drop of one, waits for the product owner's yes, while the reads run on their own as the settings say; what is proposed comes from the project's code and memory, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory, and from research only through a memory update a person approves.
- Fails if: An item taken in has no connection, place or date, differs from what the tool gave, or a read that failed was kept as if nothing happened.
- Fails if: A connected tool or forum shows a write from the project, or production was read other than through the team's own tools.
- Fails if: A key's value, or a person's name, handle or contact details, is found in the settings, a record, a log or what was taken in.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the connections the project's settings record and where each key is kept, the analytics and feedback tools the product's code and settings already name, the product's declared surfaces, the product profile's privacy level and compliance needs, where the deployment design keeps secrets, the product owner's choices of tools, forums and topics, the project's setting for when the reads run, and the records of earlier reads.
- Never read: the value of any key, except at the moment a read takes it from where it is kept, and it is never written down; anything in production other than through the tools the team already uses to look at it; and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who chooses what the project reads, says yes to each connection, and wants what consumers want and what the competition is doing understood from what really happened.
- Scope: In: choosing and connecting the tools and forums, taking in how the product is used, taking in what people say about it and its market, and updating the connections. Out: understanding what consumers want and what the competition is doing, building features or updating the product intent model, keeping the spec matched to what the agents built, promoting memory, measuring each intent, where secrets are kept, and the product profile.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A team's recipe app loads a web analytics tool in its code, sends its feedback form to a feedback tool, and is talked about in a forum where cooks compare recipe apps. The three connections are proposed, the analytics tool from the file that loads it and the forum from the product owner's word; he says yes to each, and the settings name each with where its key is kept. A week of page visits, the feedback messages and the forum's posts about recipe apps are taken in, each with its connection, where it was and when it was read, with no writer's name or email address. One day the forum cannot be read, and the record names it as not read, with the reason.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who set up the connections reads the settings and the records of the reads, and finds each connection approved by the product owner, no key's value anywhere, every item with its connection, place and date and as the tool gave it, no person's details, every failed read named, and no write in any connected tool or forum.
