Thin: proposed from the person's words; deepen with `/discover-capabilities`.

## Intent
- Goal: As the product and its tools change, a new, changed or dropped connection is proposed from what changed, the product's code and settings, its declared surfaces, the product owner's word and the reads that keep failing, each with its source; each update the product owner says yes to is recorded with the connection as it was, what changed, why and who said yes; and each connection that keeps failing is named to the product owner, so the project keeps reading the tools and forums that hold its use and what people say about it now.
- Limit: No connection is added, changed or dropped without the product owner's yes, and what held before is never written over.
- Limit: A connection that keeps failing is named, never dropped silently, and the other connections go on being read.
- Limit: The settings and records name only where each key is kept, never its value; what is proposed comes from memory first, and from research only through a memory update a person approves.
- Fails if: A connection was added, changed or dropped without the product owner's yes, or what it was before can no longer be read.
- Fails if: A connection kept failing and no one was told, or its failure stopped the reads of the others.
- Fails if: A key's value is found in the settings, a record or a log after an update.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the connections the settings record and their history, the records of earlier reads with each failure and its reason, the project's code and settings as they are now, the product's declared surfaces, and the product owner's word on what to add or drop.
- Never read: the value of any key, which stays where it is kept; anything in production other than through the tools the team already uses to look at it; and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who says yes to each update and wants to hear when a connection keeps failing.
- Scope: In: proposing, recording and keeping the history of each new, changed or dropped connection, and naming a connection that keeps failing. Out: the first connections, reading each connection, declaring surfaces, and deciding where keys are kept.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A team's planning tool gains a phone app, whose code loads the same product analytics tool for a second product in it. An update is proposed with the file that names it; the product owner says yes, and the settings keep the connection as it was and as it is now, with why. A month later the forum connection keeps failing because its key was taken away; the product owner is told which connection and why, and the analytics and feedback reads go on.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who made the updates reads the settings and the records, and finds each update approved by the product owner with its source, what held before readable with why it changed, the failing connection named to him with its reason while the others kept being read, and no key's value anywhere.
