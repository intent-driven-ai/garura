## Expectation
- Scenario: The devops engineer of a new team tool, one deployable unit with a database, sees dev, testing and pre-production proposed from the deployment design and memory, each with its own made-up data, its size, its network and who can reach it, and confirms them. The first change that passes its checks needs dev, so dev is built from the one design after the engineer's yes, and a check shows it answers. Testing and pre-production are built later, each when a change first moves on to it, from the same design, and building testing changes nothing in dev. No step reaches production. A new developer runs the tool in a dev sandbox on a laptop, with made-up data and no container platform. When the performance tests need an environment of their own, it is added before pre-production by an update, and what held before stays readable with why it changed.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who declared the environments checks each environment and property and finds behind it a source in the project or in memory, its reason and the person who confirmed it, and for each update, what held before and why it changed.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who built the environments compares each built environment with its declaration and the one design, finds no difference that a gap or an update does not explain, finds no environment built before a deployment needed it, and finds none that is production or holds data taken from production.
