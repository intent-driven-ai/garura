# A product model can be managed, and grows as plans are built

**Outcome:** a plan can be built and worked on without first writing a full product model. The model grows as the work goes, and still links up: capabilities and features to the business intents they serve, to what they need, and to what identified them; domains to business intents and capabilities.

**Why:** today a capability or feature is not "ready" until it links all the way up to a domain (ADR 043), so no play may plan or build from it. That blocks a plan built straight from a business intent. Rule from here on (the product owner, 2026-10-21): nothing is blocked.

**Asked by:** the product owner

**Proof it is met:** a capability with no domain, serving a confirmed business intent, is ready; Garura's own model is built this way for #717 and this intent.

**Must not:** change how model pages are saved.

**Identified by:** #717 — building plans showed the model must allow this.

**Plan:** this issue's number is the plan's ID — `.garura/project/plans/722.yaml`. Tracking only: a business intent holds no ICE.

**Stage:** confirmed — by the product owner, 2026-10-21
