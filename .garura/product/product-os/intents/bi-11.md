# Product model

**Outcome:** One living map of the product: an ontology that defines what a product is made of, a model defined and managed as it grows, a link to the work, and a source of new ideas and experiments.

**Why:** GitOps records changes but doesn't know what the product is. The model keeps intent connected to the product after the work closes. This is where intent-driven comes alive.

**Asked by:** the product owner

**Proof it is met:**
- A person changes the ontology through one play and the model still passes its checks.
- Any part can be added, changed or removed.
- From any intent, you can list what serves it.
- The model proposes an idea with its evidence, a person confirms it, and an experiment records the result.

**Must not:** Block work because the model is incomplete, let an agent confirm an intent, or name a plan.

**Stage:** confirmed — by the product owner, 2026-11-02
