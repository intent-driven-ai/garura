# Product → Spec

**Outcome:** What a person gives, whether a video, a prototype, a brief or all of those, becomes an understanding of the product and a spec agents can act on. The original intents are kept and stay up to date as the product evolves.

**Why:** The bottleneck is stating intent clearly and keeping it across sessions, not writing code. Most tools lose the intent once the work starts.

**Asked by:** the product owner

**Proof it is met:**
- A person confirms the intents without writing a spec.
- An agent acts on the spec without asking again.
- Everything a person gave can still be read as given.
- An intent that drifts from the product gets named.

**Must not:** Make a person write the spec, say how to build it, or block because earlier documents are missing.

**Stage:** confirmed — by the product owner, 2026-11-02
