Capability: the shaping loop (ADR 049) gets a business intent and turns it into three things, one play each: `/shape-slice` shapes a slice a person can test end to end; the plan play makes a plan that can be run and tracked; the eval play builds an eval that tests whether the business intent is met. The person approves once, at the end of the loop, before anything goes to the tracker.

## Results
1. An eval that tests whether the slice delivers its business intent, from the outside, on its surface. It tests what is known at that time (the UX design may not be known yet).
2. A plan that is ready to run and can be tracked: the epic and one story per feature, each carrying what an agent needs to build it.

## Not in this capability
- Design, build, test and validate of an epic. These moved to their own business intent (decided 2026-10-23).
