# Agents design a named piece of work, and plays then run its build

**Outcome:** The product owner names a piece of work. Agents shape it into a slice that one person can test, make its plan and write its eval. The product owner approves that design one time, and the plays then run the work.

**Why:** Today the product owner must guide each step of the design by hand. No play designs the work for Garura's own work: the product owner found this while #760 started. When agents do the design, the work is faster and it stays inside its design.

**Asked by:** the product owner

**Proof it is met:**
- A business intent goes from its issue to an approved design.
- The design has a slice that one person can test end to end on one surface, with the features it needs in build order.
- The design has a plan that a person can run and track: the plan file, one epic for the slice, and one story for each feature.
- The design has an eval that tests, from the outside, if the business intent is met.
- The product owner gives one approval, at the end.
- A play runs each step. No step is done by hand.
- The eval is kept in a place where no builder can read it.

**Must not:** Do not send the slice, the plan or the eval to the tracker before the one approval, and do not start a build before it. Do not let a builder read the eval. Do not shape an ICE that is not ready. Do not ask the person a question that the model or memory answers. Do not include the design, build, test or validation of an epic (#819), the release plays or /learn-outcomes, or the upgrade of an older product model (#835).
