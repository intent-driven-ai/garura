Title: feat: a drive finds, builds and reviews the knowledge it lacks — /search-knowledge, /build-knowledge, /review-knowledge

Kind: Story — a slice of the drives feature (#594), after #613 (the Kickoff drive).

What: today one member play, /build-memory, does every job a drive has before it asks the
person. Split it into three member plays that run in order inside every drive's rounds:
- /search-knowledge — for each open point, search the drive's sources (a prototype's views,
  journeys and page) and memory; answer with a quote or a trace.
- /build-knowledge — what search could not answer: draft researched knowledge pages as memory
  updates a person approves, draft our proposed answer for each remaining gap, and turn missing
  screens into notes for the prototype.
- /review-knowledge — present it to the person as a brief with context: what we understood, what
  we built, the few gaps each with our proposed answer; drop what the context already settles;
  record what the person approves or corrects.
The names use "knowledge", not "memory", so they do not clash with Claude Code's /memory.

Why: Kickoff run 6 (Argus mockup, 20 October) ended in an 11-question questionnaire. Kapil:
"this sort of questionnaire is not helping me as a user … present it to me like you would
present to a person who can help you close this piece … what you understood, what you created,
where the gaps are." Run 6 also cost $50.89; the change cuts the cost of a run.

Done when: ADR 042 records the split; the three plays are built, linted and tested; the shared
drive run and Kickoff run them; /build-memory is gone; the change is merged.

Out of scope: Argus showing the brief (#680, #664); promoting memory.
