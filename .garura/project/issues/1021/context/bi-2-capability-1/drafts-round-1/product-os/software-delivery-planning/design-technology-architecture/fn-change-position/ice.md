Thin: proposed from the person's words; deepen with `/discover-capabilities`.

## Intent
- Goal: When the project outgrows a technology or architecture position, a person replaces it with a new one that is in force from its confirmation, and the old one stays readable as it was, with what replaced it and why.
- Limit: A position is never edited in place; a change is a new position that replaces it.
- Limit: Only a person confirms a change, and each change names its source and its reasons.
- Limit: No work waits for a change.
- Fails if: A position's words change in place, or the old position can no longer be read.
- Fails if: A position changes with no person's confirmation, or with no reason.
- Fails if: Two positions are in force for one job after a change.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the positions in force and the ones they replaced, what changed in the project, the product profile as it is now, and memory's learnings on when each choice is outgrown and what comes next.
- Persona: the architect, who decides when the project has outgrown a position.
- Scope: In: replacing a technology or architecture position and keeping the old one with why it changed. Out: setting a first position, moving built work onto the new position, the product profile, decision records, and opening a locked design.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A product built as one deployable unit grows until several teams deploy on their own schedules; the architect confirms separate services in its place, and the old position stays readable, marked replaced, with why.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who made the change reads the positions and finds one in force for the job, and the old one as it was, with what replaced it, why and who confirmed it.
