## Intent
- Goal: When the project changes and outgrows its architecture, a person confirms an update that is in force from its confirmation, and what held before stays readable as it was, with what replaced it and why.
- Limit: Each update is proposed from Phoenix memory's architecture and technology learnings first; only when they have no answer is it researched, and the research is used only after a person approves it as a memory update. No update comes from what the AI agent knows by itself.
- Limit: The architecture is never edited in place; an update replaces what held before and keeps it.
- Limit: Only a person confirms an update, each update names its source and its reasons, and no work waits for one.
- Fails if: An architecture choice in an update is made with no memory behind it and no approved memory update.
- Fails if: The architecture's words change in place, or what held before can no longer be read.
- Fails if: The architecture changes with no person's confirmation, or with no reason.
- Fails if: Two answers are in force for one job after an update.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: Phoenix memory's architecture and technology learnings, with when each choice is outgrown and what comes after it; the architecture in force and what it replaced; what changed in the project; and the product profile as it is now.
- Persona: the architect, who decides when the project has outgrown its architecture.
- Scope: In: updating the project's architecture, with its technology, as the project changes, and keeping what held before with why it changed. Out: setting the first architecture, moving built work onto the update, the product profile, decision records, opening a locked design, and moving a memory update into Phoenix memory.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A product built as one deployable unit grows until several teams deploy on their own schedules; the agent proposes separate services from the Phoenix memory learning that says the unit is outgrown, the architect confirms it, and what held before stays readable, marked replaced, with why.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who made the update reads the architecture and finds one answer in force for the job, a Phoenix memory learning or an approved memory update behind it, and what held before as it was, with what replaced it, why and who confirmed it.
