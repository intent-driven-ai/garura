Kind: Story — a slice of the product model ontology (#597).

What: the product model rules (the ontology, the spine and the page templates) say what ADR 043 changed.
1. "No play owns a kind" (ontology, ADR 031 section 2) and "inside Kickoff each play writes one kind" are reconciled in the ontology's own text, not only in its log.
2. The Functionality kind and the functionality template say a functionality may wait in the holding folder `functionalities/` with no capability until it is linked.
