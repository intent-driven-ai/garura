# Every piece of the product model carries its approval state: approved, changed or in progress

## What this delivers
ADR 033 §1–§5, built into the ontology and the model's files. Every business intent, ICE, domain, capability, functionality, the product limits and each decision carries one approval state. "Approved" stores a fingerprint of the exact words, who approved it, when and in which review.

## Done when
- The ontology (`core/components/memory/standards/schemas/product-os/ontology.md`) names one approval lifecycle for every kind, and maps today's states onto it: an approved business intent is `confirmed`, an approved decision is `accepted`, an approved capability or functionality is `active`.
- The spine, decision and product-limit schemas, and the page templates, hold the approval fields.
- A piece approved once shows **changed** after any play edits its words, and stays **approved** when nothing touches it — worked out from the fingerprint, with no play having to remember.
- A write is never blocked (ADR 031 §2).

## Not in this story
- Agents refusing non-approved pieces — the next story.
- Who records approval outside the review step — its own spike.
- The review step that writes approval (under #594).
