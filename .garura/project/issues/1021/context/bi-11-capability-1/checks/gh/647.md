# Decide how approval is recorded outside the review step

## The question
ADR 033 says only the review step writes approval. Today three places approve outside it:
- `/intent`, run by hand, confirms a business intent at its pinned stop (ADR 031 §5);
- `/adr`, run by hand, accepts a decision on the person's typed word;
- `/shape` sets capabilities `active` with no person.

How does each record approval (fingerprint, who, when) — or stop approving?

## A starting proposal (not decided)
Any pinned yes from a person in a play also writes approval, the same way the review step does; a play never approves without a person. So `/intent` and `/adr` by hand count; `/shape` setting `active` alone does not.
