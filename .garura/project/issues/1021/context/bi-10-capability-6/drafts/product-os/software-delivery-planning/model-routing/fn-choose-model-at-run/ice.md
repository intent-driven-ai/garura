## Intent
- Goal: Once the records of runs show it pays, a router reads what each step must do at run time and chooses its model and effort, so a simple step runs fast and cheap and a hard step runs on a strong model, and each choice is named in the run's record; until then, each part runs on the tier its maker or its project names.
- Limit: No router is built before the records of runs show it pays.
- Limit: No step runs on a stronger model than its job needs.
- Limit: The fixed tier, the record and carrying a model into each coding tool stay with their owners.
- Fails if: A router chooses models while no record shows it pays.
- Fails if: A router's choice is not in the run's record.
- Fails if: A simple step runs on a strong model, or a hard step on a weak one, by the router's choice.
