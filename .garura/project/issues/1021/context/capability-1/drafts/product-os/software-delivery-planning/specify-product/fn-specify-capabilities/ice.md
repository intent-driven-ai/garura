## Intent
- Goal: Each capability the product needs has a detailed page with its features and ICE.
- Limit: A new capability comes from the person's answers or from what the screens show, never invented.
- Limit: Each ICE meets the rubric.
- Fails if: A capability has no basis, or a page fails its template, the content check or the rubric.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: what was captured, the capability's shelf, the person's answers.
- Persona: the product owner.
- Scope: In: writing each capability's full page, creating its features with their pages, setting its quality and compliance needs, and firming the product profile. Out: writing business intents, writing domain pages, and shaping slices or setting priorities.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A capability at directional depth, with no names given, comes out detailed, with at least one feature that has its own entry and page.
- Done means: A clean-context judge and the structure check.
