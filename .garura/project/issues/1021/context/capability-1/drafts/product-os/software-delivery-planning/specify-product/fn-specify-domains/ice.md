## Intent
- Goal: Each domain the product sits in has a detailed page.
- Limit: It writes domains only, and never invents one. Research must show the world recognises it.
- Fails if: A page fails its template or the content check, or it writes a capability.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the intents, what was captured, the domain shelves.
- Persona: the product owner.
- Scope: In: finding each domain the product sits in, writing its page and its index entry, and a rough product profile when none exists. Out: writing or placing capabilities and features, firming the profile, and writing business intents.

## Expectation
- Scenario: Given only a typed goal and an empty model, at least one domain gets a full page and an index entry, and no capability is written.
- Done means: A clean-context judge and the structure check.
