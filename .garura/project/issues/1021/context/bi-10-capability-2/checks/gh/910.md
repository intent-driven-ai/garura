/create-play builds --edit into each new play that writes the product model or work items
**Feature:** when `/create-play` builds a play that writes the product model or work items, it gives the play the edit standard of #904: the `--edit` command, its constraint, failure condition, recovery and scenario in the ICE, and the shared check.

**Business intent:** #904. The edit standard: `/<play> #<id> --edit: <edit instructions>`, in the person's own words; the play shows the exact list of what changes, each changed line old and new, and each other record made out of date; nothing is written before the person's yes; a shared check refuses any change the person did not name; the record keeps a history line.

**How it is built:** through the play's ICE and `/edit-play`, never as an option outside the ICE.

Filed on its own, with no parent: `/shape-slice` and `/shape-plan` place it when business intent #904 is shaped. The feature and its ICE go into the product model through `/discover-capabilities`.
