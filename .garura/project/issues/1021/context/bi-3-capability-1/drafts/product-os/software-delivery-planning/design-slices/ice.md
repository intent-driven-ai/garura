## Intent
- Goal: Each business intent is served by a slice that one person can test end to end on one surface, an ordered group of features from any capabilities in the order its tester can test them, proposed from the model and memory first and confirmed by the product owner, and each slice is updated as the project changes.
- Limit: Each part of a slice is proposed from the project's own model and records first, then from Garura memory, then from Phoenix memory; only when none of them answers is it researched, and the research is used only after a person approves it as a memory update. Nothing comes from what the AI agent knows by itself, and the person is asked only what none of them answers, never the surface or the tester.
- Limit: A slice has one surface, one tester and every feature it needs, so a person can test it with nothing from a later slice; it names the features and their order, never the steps of the build.
- Limit: It writes only the slice, its link to the business intent, its record, the parts the slice needs with a thin ICE where one is missing, and the intent's page when the model has none; never a plan, an epic, a story or the eval.
- Limit: Nothing is kept before the product owner's typed yes; a slice whose work has started changes only through an edit the person approves, and an intent that a slice already serves gets no second slice.
- Fails if: A slice is written with no surface, more than one surface or no tester, or a person cannot test it end to end without a later slice.
- Fails if: The person is asked a question the model or memory answers, or about the surface or the tester, or a proposed answer has no source.
- Fails if: A second slice is made for an intent that a slice not in progress already serves, or a slice in progress is reshaped without an edit the person approved.
- Fails if: Something is kept without the product owner's yes, or a part the slice needs is skipped.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the business intent's page, or its issue when the model has none; the model's domains, capabilities and features with their ICE; the slices that exist and the status of their epics; the domain shelves that place the intent's outcome in its field; the declared surfaces and the project's architecture; and the questions already asked and answered in the issue's short-term memory.
- Never read: the slice's eval files. Only their names are used, to say which a change may make out of date.
- Persona: the product owner, who names the business intent, answers only what the model and memory cannot, and approves each slice; and the tester each slice names.
- Scope: In: designing the slice that serves a business intent, adding what it needs to the model, writing it with its link, using again a slice that serves the intent, and updating or taking out a slice. Out: the hidden file and the eval, the epic and the stories, a feature found during work, how the slice looks and works on its surface, the project's surfaces, architecture, deployment and environments, the ICE itself, the order of the work, the build, retiring an intent, and moving a memory update into Phoenix memory.

## Expectation
- Scenario: The product owner of a clinic's booking tool names the business intent "patients book their own appointments". The booking screen is proposed as the surface and a patient as the tester, with "pick a free slot" and then "confirm the booking", from two capabilities, and paying for the visit left out with why. One feature has no ICE, so a thin one is proposed and the brief warns of it. The brief asks one question that no memory answers. After the product owner's yes, one slice serves the intent, and a second run for the same intent makes no new slice.
- Done means: The model check, the write guard and the approval record, each read by a script and not by the agent that designed the slice, show one surface, a tester and the features in build order, the link to the intent, a typed yes, and no plan, epic, story or eval written.
- Done means: A person who did not design the slice reads it and names, for each feature, what the tester does on the surface to test it, and finds nothing the slice needs from a later slice.
