## Intent
- Goal: From the findings the second capability of Run → Product hands on, the change each one calls for is worked out on its own, with the findings and items it rests on, why, how sure and who decides; each change is recommended to the product owner, or goes ahead on its own only where his setting allows its kind and shown to him at once with a way back; and each change he approves or allows is started down the path that builds it, through the part that owns each step, and followed until it lands or is turned down, so that what the product becomes next follows what its consumers want.
- Limit: It never changes an intent's words, proof, must-not or stage, never confirms, retires or replaces one, and never lets these, a new business intent, new words on a page that exists, or taking a part out go ahead on its own.
- Limit: It writes no page, index entry, work item or code itself, and every change rests on the findings the second capability handed on, never on what the AI agent knows by itself.
- Limit: Only the kinds of change the product owner allowed in the project's settings go ahead on their own, and never one that rests on a weak finding, a finding against an intent, or only on what competitors do.
- Fails if: A recommendation or a change names no finding, or rests on an item that is not in the findings handed on.
- Fails if: An intent's words, proof, must-not or stage changed, or a part was retired or taken out, without a person's name.
- Fails if: A change went ahead on its own when the setting did not allow its kind, or was not shown to the product owner with a way back.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the findings the second capability handed on, with their items, counts, weak marks and places; the intent pages, the model's index and its capability and feature pages; the project's setting for which kinds of change may go ahead on their own; the earlier recommendations and the product owner's answers; and where each change stands at the part that owns it.
- Never read: the connected tools and forums themselves, production, anything that would bring back who a person is, and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who decides what the product becomes next, chooses which kinds of change may go ahead on their own, and wants each change checkable against what it rests on.
- Scope: In: working out the change each finding calls for, recommending it and keeping the answer, letting allowed kinds go ahead on their own with a way back, starting each change through its owning part, and updating the recommendations. Out: connecting and understanding, writing pages, links or work items, making and running the plan, designing and building, fixing defects, judging or measuring an intent, and promoting memory.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A clinic's booking app gets its findings for the month. A strong want for evening appointments is placed on the booking capability, which serves a confirmed intent, and no feature meets it; the project allows a new feature under such a capability to go ahead on its own, so the feature is handed to its owning part, marked as set going by this capability and waiting for review, and the product owner sees it at once with its items and a way back; the plan session still asks his yes before the model and the tracker change. Paying when booking, a weak possible new need, becomes a recommendation, and he turns it down with his reason, which is kept. A finding against the intent that bookings close a day ahead becomes a question for him. No intent's words changed.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who proposed the changes opens each recommendation and each change, finds its findings and items back it, finds every change that went ahead on its own of an allowed kind and shown with a way back, every turned-down recommendation kept with the reason given, and no intent's words or stage, page, index entry, work item or code written by this capability.
