## Intent
- Goal: A change whose kind the product owner allowed in the project's settings, and which rests on a want of the product's own consumers that is not weak, goes against no intent and rests on more than what competitors do, goes ahead on its own: it is handed to its owning part marked as set going by this capability and waiting for review, shown to him at once with its findings, items and a way back, and stopped for good when he sets its kind back to recommend, with the record of his answers by kind shown beside the setting, so that the work he trusts the agent with is done for him and nothing changes without him seeing it.
- Limit: Every kind starts as recommend, and a new business intent, an intent's words, proof, must-not or stage, retiring or replacing an intent, new words on a page that exists, and taking a part out can never be set to go ahead on their own.
- Limit: Nothing goes ahead on its own from a weak finding, a finding against an intent, or only what competitors do, and each owning part keeps its own rules.
- Limit: Each change that goes ahead is shown at once with a way back, and setting a kind back to recommend stops it at once.
- Fails if: A change went ahead on its own when its kind was not allowed, or was of a kind that can never go ahead.
- Fails if: A change went ahead on its own from a weak finding, a finding against an intent, or a competitor alone.
- Fails if: A change that went ahead was not shown to the product owner at once with its findings and a way back.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the project's setting for which kinds of change may go ahead on their own, the changes worked out with their findings and weak marks, the intent pages and the model's index, and the product owner's earlier answers by kind.
- Never read: the connected tools and forums themselves, production, anything that would bring back who a person is, and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who chooses which kinds of change may go ahead on their own and wants to see each one at once, with a way back.
- Scope: In: reading the setting, letting allowed changes go ahead on their own, showing each with a way back, stopping a kind set back, and keeping the record of answers by kind. Out: working out the changes, making them, and where settings live.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A clinic's booking app allows new features under capabilities that serve a confirmed intent. A strong want for evening appointments is placed on the booking capability; the feature goes ahead, marked as set going by this capability and waiting for review, and the product owner sees it the same day with its items and how to take it back. A want for a dark screen rests on two posts and stays a recommendation. A change to the intent that bookings close a day ahead stays a recommendation, whatever the setting. He then sets new features back to recommend, and the next one waits for him.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who let the changes go ahead compares each one with the setting, its findings and the time he was shown it, and finds every one of an allowed kind, none from a weak finding, a finding against an intent or a competitor alone, each shown at once with a way back, and none of a kind after he set it back.
