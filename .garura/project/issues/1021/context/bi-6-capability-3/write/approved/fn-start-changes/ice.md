## Intent
- Goal: Each change the product owner approved, or that went ahead on its own, is handed to the part that owns its first step, a new feature to Add a feature found during work or to Specify the product, new words or an intent change to Update the spec to match what's built, a retirement to Replace or retire an intent or Manage the model, a surface or architecture change to its design capability, with its findings whole and the words approved, who approved them and where, and is followed until its owner lands it or turns it down, so that building a feature or updating the product model is done by the parts that already do it and the product owner sees where each change stands.
- Limit: It writes no page, index entry, work item or code, and does not choose the order of building.
- Limit: Each owner's rules stand: where an owner asks a person's yes, the change waits there.
- Limit: A change its owner turns down comes back to the product owner as a recommendation, with the owner's reason.
- Fails if: A change reached a part that does not own its first step, or reached no part.
- Fails if: This capability wrote a page, an index entry, a work item or code.
- Fails if: A change's record does not say where it stands at its owner.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the approved and allowed changes with their findings and approved words, the model's index and the capability pages that say which part owns each step, the plan files that hold each intent's work, and where each change stands at its owner.
- Never read: the connected tools and forums themselves, production, anything that would bring back who a person is, a plan's evals folder, and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who wants each approved change built by the parts that build, and to see where it stands.
- Scope: In: handing each change to the part that owns its first step and following it until it lands or is turned down. Out: writing pages, links or work items, making and running the plan, designing, building and fixing.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A school's timetable app has three changes. New words for the timetable feature's page, approved by the product owner, go to Update the spec to match what's built and land there. A new business intent for weekly changes, in his own words, goes to Specify the product. A new feature for lesson swap requests, under a capability whose intent has a plan, goes to Add a feature found during work, where the plan session asks his yes; he turns it down there, and it comes back to him as a recommendation with his reason. This capability wrote nothing in the model or the tracker.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who started the changes checks each change's record against its owner's records, and finds each one at the part that owns its first step, with its findings and approved words, its state right, and no page, index entry, work item or code written by this capability.
