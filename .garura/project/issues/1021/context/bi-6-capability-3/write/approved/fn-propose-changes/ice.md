## Intent
- Goal: For each finding the second capability of Run → Product hands on, the change it calls for is worked out on its own, a new feature under a capability, new words on a capability or feature page, a new business intent or new words on one, a part retired, or no change yet, each with its kind, its part, its draft words, the findings it rests on whole, why, how sure as facts, who decides and which part would make it, so that every change to the product starts from what its consumers want and can be checked.
- Limit: Every change names the findings it rests on, whole, and every finding handed on is under a change or listed as calling for none yet, with why.
- Limit: A new business intent is only recommended for the product owner to give in his own words, a finding against an intent becomes a question and never a change, and no change rests on what competitors do alone.
- Limit: It writes no page, link or work item; words for a page are a draft for the part that owns it.
- Fails if: A change names no finding, or a finding handed on is neither under a change nor listed with why.
- Fails if: A change to an intent was proposed from a finding against it, or a new intent's words were drafted as the product owner's.
- Fails if: A change rests only on what a competitor is doing.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the findings handed on with their items, counts, weak marks and places, the intent pages, the model's index, the capability and feature pages, and the earlier changes with the product owner's answers.
- Never read: the connected tools and forums themselves, production, anything that would bring back who a person is, and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who wants each change to the product to start from what consumers want, with why and how sure.
- Scope: In: working out the change each finding calls for, with its findings, why, how sure, who decides and which part would make it. Out: drawing and placing the findings, showing and deciding the changes, writing any page, link or work item, and defects or parts no page names.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A recipe app has four findings. The want for a shopping list grouped by shop aisle is placed on the shopping list feature, so new words for that feature's page are proposed as a draft, naming the want and its items. A want for meal plans fits no part, so a new business intent is recommended for the product owner to give in his own words. A finding that people praise a rival's pantry tracker, which no want of the app's own users matches, leads to no change. A want resting on two posts is listed as calling for no change yet, because it is weak.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who worked out the changes reads each change against its findings and items and finds it follows from them, finds every finding under a change or listed with why, no change to an intent proposed from a finding against it, and no change resting on a competitor alone.
