Thin: proposed from the person's words; deepen with `/discover-capabilities`.

## Intent
- Goal: Each change that may not go ahead on its own is shown to the product owner as a recommendation, in one current list with what waits, what he answered and, apart, what went ahead on its own, each with its findings whole, their items, why, how sure, who decides and what it would set going; his answer, approve, change or turn down, is kept word for word with who, when and where; an approved change goes on with exactly the words he approved, and a turned-down one is kept and raised again only when its findings move, so that such a change starts only from his word.
- Limit: Every recommendation shows the findings it rests on, with their items, and the list is ordered by facts a person can check.
- Limit: What goes on is exactly what he approved, and a turned-down recommendation is never raised again with nothing new.
- Limit: An unanswered recommendation stops no other recommendation or change.
- Fails if: A recommendation is shown without its findings and items, or his answer is not kept word for word with who, when and where.
- Fails if: What went on differs from what he approved.
- Fails if: A turned-down recommendation was raised again when its findings had not moved.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the changes worked out with their findings, the changes that went ahead on their own, the product owner's earlier answers, and the second capability's report.
- Never read: the connected tools and forums themselves, production, anything that would bring back who a person is, and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who decides each recommendation and wants it checkable against what it rests on.
- Scope: In: showing each recommendation, keeping the product owner's answer, sending an approved change on, and keeping a turned-down one. Out: working out the changes, letting a change go ahead on its own, making the change, and building the review tool.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A shop's checkout has three recommendations. He approves new words on the delivery cost feature's page after changing one sentence, and the change goes on with his sentence. He turns down a new feature for gift wrapping, saying it is not for this year; next month the same findings come, and it is not raised again. In the third month many more items ask for gift wrapping, and it is raised again, showing what moved. A question on an intent waits unanswered, and the other two went on without it.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who made the list compares each recommendation with its findings and items, each answer with what the product owner typed, and each approved change with what went on, and finds them the same, with no turned-down recommendation raised again while its findings stood still.
