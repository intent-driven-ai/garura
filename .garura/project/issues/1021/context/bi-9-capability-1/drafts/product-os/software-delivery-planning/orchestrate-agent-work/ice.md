## Intent
- Goal: A person chooses what is delivered next as a slice, sees every plan, each plan's branch against main and one plan as a tree from the plan files and git alone, sets each item's status and each plan's Status and Direction in one approved session on the plan's own branch, learns at the end of every run what comes next, and removes a plan on their own approval; every status and direction is one a person set, and after each session the plan file and the tracker agree.
- Limit: Nothing here works out a status or the direction from the work under it; an item whose own issue closed changes here only through the session's list and the person's "approved".
- Limit: No view reads the tracker or the product model or calls a model, and no view writes a plan file, an issue or a branch.
- Limit: Nothing lands in a session before a plain "approved", what lands is exactly the list the person saw, and a plan is changed only on its own branch.
- Limit: No plan, and no plan's status, direction or branch, is written in the product model.
- Fails if: A view shows a status, a direction or a branch that is not the plan file's or git's, or two prints of the same files differ.
- Fails if: A status or the direction changes that no person set, or the plan file and the tracker disagree after a session.
- Fails if: A slice is written with no surface or no tester, a shaping run changes a slice in progress, or a plan is removed without the name of the person who approved.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the plan files and the names of their folders, git's branches and their commits against main, the plan-view and plan-update templates, the plan's issues as the tracker holds them, the Next-line map, the product model's index and pages for the slice, the question setting, and the person's words and answers.
- Never read: any memory file by its path, any ICE page, and any plan's evals folder.
- Persona: the product owner, who chooses the slice, sets the order and status of the work and approves each session; and the developer, a person or an AI agent who builds the code, who reads where a plan stands and what comes next.
- Scope: In: choosing the slice, the three views of the plans, the statuses and direction of a session, getting onto a plan's branch and back, the Next line, and removing a plan; later, choosing the next item, working out what a plan waits on, and pausing a plan. Out: making and changing work items, cutting and landing a plan's branch, the hidden file and the evals, the loops, the tracker writes, the decision records, the way past each gate, the settings and names, and the product model.

## Expectation
- Scenario: In a clinic's booking app, the product owner runs /shape-slice for the intent that patients book their own appointments online; the brief proposes the self-booking slice on the booking screen, tested by a patient, with pick a free slot before confirm the booking, and the product owner types yes. Once its plan exists, a developer back after two days runs /show-plan on a story branch and sees two stories open and the rest counted; /list-plans shows the plan In Progress, ⏵, on its branch; /list-plans --graph shows it 3 ahead and 0 behind. The product owner then runs /update-plan, says one story is done and the plan is paused, and types approved: the story's issue closes, the plan file reads that story Done and the plan Paused, one commit lands on the plan's branch, and the session ends on the branch it started on. In a shop's checkout project, a plan file holds a status the template does not know, and each view stops and names that file.
- Done means: Someone other than the product owner and the developer reads the model's index, the plan files, git, the tracker and the session's commit, and finds one slice with one surface and one tester that serves the intent, every view equal to what the plan files and git say, the story's issue closed with only its own item Done, the plan Paused and no other status changed, one commit on the plan's branch that names what was asked, the session back on its starting branch, and, for the shop's file, no view printed.
