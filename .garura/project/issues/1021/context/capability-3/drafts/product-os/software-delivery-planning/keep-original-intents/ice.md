## Intent
- Goal: Each business intent stays a true record of what the person wants: in the words they approved, and in step with the product as it changes.
- Limit: An intent's words change only through words a person approved, and only a person confirms or drops an intent.
- Limit: A change to an intent's words keeps its record of what was done.
- Limit: Nothing is removed while a part still names it, and every removal names the person who approved it.
- Limit: Drift is named to a person, never fixed in silence.
- Fails if: An intent's words change with no person's approval behind them.
- Fails if: A part serves an intent and the intent's page does not show it, or the page shows a part that does not serve it.
- Fails if: An intent no longer fits the product and nothing names the drift.
- Fails if: Something a person gave can no longer be read as it was given, and nothing says so.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the intent pages, the model's index and the intents it links to each part, the Sources and their capture records, the intents tracker setting, and the rules of the Business Intent and Source kinds.
- Persona: the product owner, who confirmed the intents and wants them kept as they said them.
- Scope: In: changing intent pages from approved words, keeping what each intent touches, replacing and retiring intents, where intents are kept, naming drift, and reading back what was given. Out: capturing Sources and drafting intents, the link rules, merging capabilities and removing slices, and drift with no intent in it.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A capability is linked to an intent, and the intent's page then lists that capability with its mark.
- Done means: The gap finder, run over the whole model by someone other than the one who linked the parts, reports no link to an intent held on one side only.
- Done means: A person who did not write the pages reads an intent next to what was given, long after it was confirmed, and both still say what they said.
