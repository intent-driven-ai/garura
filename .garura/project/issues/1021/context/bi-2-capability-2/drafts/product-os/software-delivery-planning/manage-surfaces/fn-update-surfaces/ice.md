Thin: proposed from the person's words; deepen with `/discover-capabilities`.

## Intent
- Goal: When the project gains or drops a surface, or a surface's kind, properties or guidelines must change, a person confirms an update that is in force from its confirmation, and what held before stays readable as it was, with what replaced it and why.
- Limit: Each update is proposed from the project's own memory and code first, then from Garura memory, then from Phoenix memory; only when none of them answers is it researched, and the research is used only after a person approves it as a memory update. No update comes from what the AI agent knows by itself.
- Limit: A declared surface is never edited in place; an update replaces what held before and keeps it.
- Limit: Only a person confirms an update, each update names its reason and its source, and no work waits for one.
- Fails if: A declared surface's words change in place, or what held before can no longer be read.
- Fails if: A surface, a property or a guideline changes with no person's confirmation, or with no reason.
- Fails if: Two answers are in force for one property or guideline of a surface after an update.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the surfaces in force, with their properties and guidelines, and what they replaced; what changed in the project, such as a new app, new screen sizes or a new component library in its code, a slice on a surface that is not declared, a changed set-up answer, or a quality level that rose in the product profile; the Phoenix memory learnings a property or a guideline names as its source; and the slices designed on a surface the update changes.
- Persona: the product owner, who decides when the project's surfaces, their properties or their guidelines change.
- Scope: In: updating the project's surfaces, their properties and their guidelines as the project changes, and keeping what held before with why it changed. Out: declaring the first surfaces, moving slices already designed or built onto an updated surface, the technology of a new surface or design system, raising a quality level in the product profile, decision records, opening a locked design, and moving a memory update into Phoenix memory.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A team tool's browser page, declared for desktop screens, must now serve phones. The agent proposes the new screen sizes from the pages the code now serves and the Phoenix memory learning for a browser surface, the product owner confirms the update with its reason, and what held before stays readable, marked replaced, with why it changed.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who made the update reads the surface and finds one answer in force for each property and guideline, the source and the reason behind the update, the person who confirmed it, and what held before as it was.
