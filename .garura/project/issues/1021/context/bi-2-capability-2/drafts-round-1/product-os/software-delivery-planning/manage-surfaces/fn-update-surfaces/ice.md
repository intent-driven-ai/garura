Thin: proposed from the person's words; deepen with `/discover-capabilities`.

## Intent
- Goal: When the project gains or drops a surface, or a surface must change its kind, a person confirms an update that is in force from its confirmation, and what held before stays readable as it was, with what replaced it and why.
- Limit: Each update is proposed from the project's own memory and code first, then from Garura memory, then from Phoenix memory; only when none of them answers is it researched, and the research is used only after a person approves it as a memory update. No update comes from what the AI agent knows by itself.
- Limit: The surfaces are never edited in place; an update replaces what held before and keeps it.
- Limit: Only a person confirms an update, each update names its reason and its source, and no work waits for one.
- Fails if: The surfaces' words change in place, or what held before can no longer be read.
- Fails if: The surfaces change with no person's confirmation, or with no reason.
- Fails if: Two kinds are in force for one surface after an update.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the surfaces in force and what they replaced; what changed in the project, such as a new app or new pages in its code, a slice on a surface that is not on the list, or a set-up answer that changed; and the slices designed on a surface the update changes.
- Persona: the product owner, who decides when the project's surfaces change.
- Scope: In: updating the project's surfaces as the project changes, and keeping what held before with why it changed. Out: declaring the first surfaces, moving slices already designed or built onto an updated surface, the technology of a new surface, decision records, opening a locked design, and moving a memory update into Phoenix memory.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A team tool declared with only a command line gains a browser page for team leads who are not technical. The agent proposes the new surface from the pages the code now serves, the product owner confirms it with its reason, and the list that held before stays readable, marked replaced, with why it changed.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who made the update reads the surfaces and finds one kind in force for each surface, the source and the reason behind the update, the person who confirmed it, and what held before as it was.
