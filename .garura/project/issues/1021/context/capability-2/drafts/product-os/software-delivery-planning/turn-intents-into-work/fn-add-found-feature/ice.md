## Intent
- Goal: A feature found during work joins the product model under its capability with a thin ICE, and joins its slice and its epic with its story, on one approval.
- Limit: Nothing reaches the model, the tracker or the plan file before "approved".
- Limit: The model is written only through the model's own skills.
- Fails if: The feature lands in the model but not in the tracker or the plan file, or the other way round.
- Fails if: It lands without its thin ICE, or with only one of the two thin marks.
- Fails if: The folder that holds the evals is written, or what is owed is not named.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the person's words, the model's index and its capabilities, the slices that serve the intent, the plan file and the tracker.
- Never read: the folder that holds the evals.
- Persona: the product owner, or a builder, who finds the feature during work.
- Scope: In: placing the feature, its thin page and model entry, and its story under the epic. Out: a new slice, the hidden-file entry, the eval and deepening the ICE.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A session that names one new feature, approved once, ends with the feature in the model, in its slice and under its epic.
- Done means: The agreement check reads ok: the plan file, the tracker and the model agree, the feature's page, entry, thin marks and story are there, and the folder that holds the evals is unchanged.
