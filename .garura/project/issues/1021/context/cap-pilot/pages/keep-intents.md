# Capability: Keep the original business intents

To be deepened with `/discover-capabilities`. Everything below is a directional proposal, drafted from the product owner's one line and the words of business intent bi-1.

## Directional intent
Everything a person gives is kept exactly as given, and the intents stay up to date as the product evolves, with no drift.

This capability serves the business intent Product → Spec (bi-1), where what a person gives, whether a video, a prototype, a brief or all of those, becomes an understanding of the product and a spec agents can act on. Its rough scope is the keeping: what a person gave can still be read as given, and an intent that drifts from the product gets named. Understanding the product and turning intents into agent work are other capabilities under the same intent.

It matters because the bottleneck is stating intent clearly and keeping it across sessions, not writing code.

What makes it unique: most tools lose the intent once the work starts; here everything a person gives is kept as given, and the intents stay up to date with no drift.

Features: none written yet. The product owner creates them later.
