## Intent
- Goal: Once the tools have run and the walk is done, one user experience verdict for the built work gives each property and guideline of each surface, and each screen of the screen design at each size the surface names, a pass or a fail with how and where it was checked, names each part not tested or not walked with its reason, is a pass only when every part passes, and says so plainly when nothing is declared to test against; and each finding goes back as a handoff plan with what a person meets, where, at which size and the way back: to the developer through Build the work test first when the builder can fix it alone, to the product owner when a person must decide, and to Fix defects test first when the work was already accepted.
- Limit: The verdict takes the records of the review of each change and of the build's separate test run as evidence without running them again, and it is kept where no part that builds the work reads it.
- Limit: A handoff plan never names the test, its rule, its id or its tool, never gives a figure measured or a picture the test kept, and its way back is guidance, never code.
- Limit: A finding goes to the developer only when its way back is a change of the code the builder controls; one that changes the design system, the screen design, a part the builder does not control, a surface's declaration, property or guideline, a departure with no confirmed update, one not walked because no person was named, or one in doubt goes to the product owner.
- Fails if: The verdict is a pass while a part failed, was not tested or was not walked, or nothing declared to test against is read as a pass.
- Fails if: A handoff plan names the test, its rule, its id or its tool, or gives a figure or a picture, or the builder was given the verdict or a walk's record.
- Fails if: A finding that needs a person's judgment or authority, such as a change of the design system or the screen design, went to the developer, or a finding was let through with neither a fix nor a confirmed update.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the results of the tools and of the walk with how and where each part was checked, at which size, and who walked, each part not tested or not walked, the declared surfaces with their properties and guidelines and their confirmed updates, the screen design in force with its confirmed updates, what the builder controls, the records of the review of each change and of the build's separate test run, whether the work was already accepted, and the recovery rule.
- Never read: the value of any secret, such as a password or a key; anything in production, such as its data or its traffic; and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who reads the verdict and decides each finding a person must decide; the developer gets back, as a handoff plan, each finding the builder can fix alone.
- Scope: In: the one verdict against each surface's properties and guidelines and the screen design, and writing and sending each handoff plan. Out: opening the screens and walking the build, fixing, updating a surface or the screen design, recording a defect in the tracker, and running the tests again.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A clinic's booking page is tested. The verdict fails two parts and names a third, the phone layout of the confirmation screen, as not walked, because no person was named for this run. The developer gets a handoff plan: the button that confirms a visit is not the design system's main button that the screen design shows; it must be. The product owner gets a second finding, that the screen design's calendar cannot fit the smallest screen size the surface names, whose way back is an update of the screen design, and the part not walked, each with how and where it was checked. No handoff plan names a test, a tool, a rule or a figure, or holds a picture.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who gave the verdict reads it beside the results, the walk's record, the surface and the screen design, and finds every part with a pass, a fail or a reason it was not tested or not walked, each with how and where it was checked, no pass while a part failed or was not tested, each handoff plan with what a person meets, where and the way back, sent where the recovery rule's test says, and no test, tool, rule, figure or picture in any of them.
