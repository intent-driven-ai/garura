## Intent
- Goal: Each finding about what consumers want or what the competition is doing is placed, on its own, on the business intents, capabilities and features of the product model it bears on, each with why and the page lines it rests on, or named as fitting none, a possible new need, or as against an intent, with both sides; and a defect or a part no page names is named for its owner, Fix defects test first or Keep the spec matched to what's built, so that each finding reaches the part of the product it is about.
- Limit: It places and never links: no link, Touches list, index entry, page or intent changes.
- Limit: A finding that fits no part is named as fitting none, never pushed onto the nearest part, and a finding against an intent is never settled here.
- Limit: A defect and a part no page names are never counted as wants.
- Fails if: A finding is placed on a part with no why or no page lines, or a finding that fits no part was placed on one.
- Fails if: A defect or a part no page names was counted as a want, or not named for its owner.
- Fails if: The model shows a link, Touches list, index entry, page or intent changed by this placing.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the findings drawn from the items, the intent pages, the model's index, the capability and feature pages, and the earlier placings.
- Never read: the connected tools and forums themselves, production, anything that would bring back who a person is, and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who wants each finding placed on the part of the product it is about, with why.
- Scope: In: placing each finding on intents, capabilities and features, naming what fits none or goes against an intent, and naming a defect or a part no page names for its owner. Out: drawing the findings, linking parts or changing pages, writing an intent, fixing a defect, and deciding what to build.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A recipe app has three findings. The want for a shopping list grouped by shop aisle is placed on the shopping list feature, its capability and the intent they serve, each with the page lines. Users who say the shopping list loses items when the phone is offline are named as a defect on the shopping list feature, for Fix defects test first, and not counted as a want. Cooks who praise a rival's meal planner fit no part of the model, and the finding is named as a possible new need. The model is unchanged.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who placed the findings reads each place against the page lines it names and finds it right, finds every finding that fits no part named as fitting none, every defect and part no page names named for its owner, and no change in the model.
