## Intent
- Goal: When the findings are drawn or change, the product owner is shown, on their own as the project's setting says, one current report of what consumers want and what the competition is doing, every finding with the items it rests on, its counts, how sure it is and where it is placed, the weak findings in a part of their own, the defects and the parts no page names apart with their owners, and what is new or has moved marked; and the same findings are handed, whole, to the next capability of Run → Product, so that what needs to be done next starts from them.
- Limit: Every finding is shown with what it rests on; a weak one is shown as weak and never left out, and the order follows facts a person can check.
- Limit: Nothing waits for a yes, because nothing outside the findings changes; the report says what is wanted and never what to do.
- Limit: The next capability gets the same findings as the report, never a summary of them.
- Fails if: A finding, or a weak one, is missing from the report, or a weak one is shown as strong.
- Fails if: What was handed to the next capability differs from the report in a finding, item, count, weak mark or place.
- Fails if: The report asks for a yes on anything but a competitor to follow, or recommends a change.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the placed findings with their items, counts and weak marks, the earlier report, and the project's setting for when the report reaches the product owner.
- Never read: the connected tools and forums themselves, production, anything that would bring back who a person is, and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who reads what consumers want and what the competition is doing, each finding checkable against its items.
- Scope: In: showing the findings to the product owner as one current report with what changed marked, and handing them whole to the next capability. Out: drawing and placing the findings, recommending or making a change, and building the review tool.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A school's timetable app has its report for the month. The want for a note when a lesson moves rests on more items than last month and is marked as moved, with both counts; a want for a dark screen rests on two posts and sits in the weak part; a defect on the timetable's print view is listed apart, named for Fix defects test first. The next capability receives the same findings, item for item.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who made the report compares it with the placed findings and with what the next capability received, and finds every finding in both, each with its items, the weak ones marked weak, what moved marked, no recommendation, and no yes asked except for a competitor to follow.
