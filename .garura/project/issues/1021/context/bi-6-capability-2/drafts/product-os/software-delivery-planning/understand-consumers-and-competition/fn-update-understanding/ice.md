Thin: proposed from the person's words; deepen with `/discover-capabilities`.

## Intent
- Goal: As new items come in, the product model changes, or the product owner changes which competitors are followed, the findings are updated on their own: new items rest under the wants they bear on or start new ones, a want that stops coming up is marked as fading, the places a model change touches are worked out again with why, and a competitor to add or drop is proposed for the product owner's yes; what held before stays readable with what changed and why, so the understanding stays current and shows how each want moved.
- Limit: What held before is never written over or deleted; a fading want stays listed, and what was found about a dropped competitor stays readable.
- Limit: No competitor is added or dropped without the product owner's yes; every other update runs without waiting for a person.
- Limit: It writes only its own findings: no intent, page, link, memory, tracker or connected tool changes, and what is proposed comes from memory first and from research only through a memory update a person approves.
- Fails if: A finding's earlier state, or why it changed, can no longer be read, or a want was deleted instead of marked as fading.
- Fails if: A finding stays placed on a part that the model merged or retired.
- Fails if: A competitor was added or dropped without the product owner's yes.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the new items and the records of their reads, the earlier findings and their places, the product model as it is now, the competitors followed, the product owner's word on a competitor, and the project's setting for when a want is fading.
- Never read: the connected tools and forums themselves, production, anything that would bring back who a person is, and any ICE page.
- Persona: the product owner, who wants the understanding kept current and says yes to each change in the competitors followed.
- Scope: In: updating each finding as items come in and the model changes, marking fading wants, working out places again, proposing competitors to add or drop, and keeping what held before. Out: drawing the first findings, changing the model, and changing the connections.

## Expectation
- Scenario: A team's planning tool merges two capabilities into one. The wants placed on the old capability are placed again on the merged one, each with why, and their old places stay readable. A month of new items adds to the want for a calendar view, and its count before and after are both shown. A want for a print button has no new item for the time the project sets and is marked as fading, still listed. A rival no one names any more is proposed to drop, and stays followed until the product owner says yes.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who made the updates reads the findings and their history, and finds every earlier state readable with why it changed, no finding placed on a merged or retired part, fading wants still listed, and each change in the competitors with the product owner's yes.
