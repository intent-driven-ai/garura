## Intent
- Goal: The gaps inside the model are found and closed: the gap finder lists loose parts, parents that do not exist, capabilities with no feature, intents nothing serves, parts that serve nothing, links kept on one side only, empty domains, likely duplicates and screens no page covers, each with the link to make and why or the play that does the work; and the gap closer makes what the model can take with no new words, both sides of each link, and hands the rest back as work.
- Limit: The gap finder only reads, and the gap closer writes no new words and deletes only a duplicate marked agreed.
- Limit: No link and no merge without a basis from the pages, their sources, the capture or the write-up; a parent is never made up.
- Limit: A part that already has a parent is never moved by the gap closer.
- Fails if: A loose part, a one-sided link or an intent nothing serves is in the model and not on the gap finder's list.
- Fails if: The gap closer writes a link on one side only, or merges two parts that were not marked agreed.
- Fails if: A link or a merge is made with no reason given.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the model's index, its pages and their sources, the intent pages' Touches lists, a prototype's capture when there is one, the gap finder's candidates and its output shape, the gap closer's order and refusals, and where the loop runs them.
- Never read: any memory file by its path, any ICE page, and any file in a plan's evals folder.
- Persona: the technical lead, who reads the gaps and agrees the duplicates; and the product owner, who decides the missing work.
- Scope: In: finding loose parts, missing parents, missing features, intents nothing serves, one-sided links, empty domains, duplicates and uncovered screens, and closing what the model can take. Out: the shape check of each page, taking out a part on approval, the discovery work handed on, drift from what was built, and running a loop's rounds.

## Expectation
- Scenario: In a shop's checkout service, after a round, the gap finder lists Saved cards as loose with the Payments domain proposed and why, and one capability that serves "shoppers pay fast" whose intent page does not list it; the gap closer files Saved cards under Payments and writes the Touches line. In a news site's publishing tool, two capabilities show the same screen and purpose and are marked agreed; the gap closer keeps one with the other's intents and features.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who ran the round reads the gap list, the closer's report, the index and the intent pages, and finds every loose part and one-sided link of the model on the list with a reason, the shop's Saved cards under Payments, the Touches line written, the news site's one capability holding both sets of intents and features, and no link or merge made without a reason.
