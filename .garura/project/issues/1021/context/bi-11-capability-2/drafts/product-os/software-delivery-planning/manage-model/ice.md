## Intent
- Goal: The product model stays one living map of the product as work is done: any part can be added, changed or removed through the keeper and its skills, from words a person approved, all or nothing; its links follow the ontology's rules and are kept on both sides; its checks run on every change and give findings, never a stop; from any intent a person can list what serves it; each part carries its approval state; and the model proposes what to try next with its evidence, and records each experiment's result.
- Limit: The model changes only through a skill, never by hand, and a take-out names the person who approved it.
- Limit: No rule, check or missing link stops a write, and a missing domain, a missing link, a thin page or a part found on its own never stops work.
- Limit: Only a person confirms a business intent, and no model page or index entry names a plan.
- Limit: The ontology, drift from what was built, choosing a slice and the way past a gate stay with Define what a product is made of, Keep the spec matched to what's built, Orchestrate agent work through planning and Give every gate a way past.
- Fails if: A part is added, changed or removed by hand, or a write leaves the index and the pages disagreeing.
- Fails if: A part is removed with no person's name, or a page or an entry still names a part that is gone.
- Fails if: Work stops because the model is incomplete, or a part found on its own is counted as a gap.
- Fails if: A link written by a change is kept on one side only, a feature needs another feature, or a model page or entry names a plan.

## Context
- Read: Gather it from the memory layer. Look first at the project's existing memory and its existing implementation, then Garura memory, then Phoenix memory. If none of them answers, research it and raise a memory update for a person to approve. For this part, look for: the ontology's kinds and link rules, the model's index and pages, the keeper and its skills (the page skill, the two index skills, the intent-page skill, the gap finder and the gap closer) with their scripts and tests, the ready check and the structure check with their last runs, the decisions on who writes the index and on approval in the model, the approved pages that hand work here, and the issues that plan the rest.
- Never read: any memory file by its path, any ICE page, and any file in a plan's evals folder.
- Persona: the product owner, who confirms intents and approves each change and take-out; the technical lead, who changes the model's structure; and the developer, a person or an AI agent who builds the code, who finds parts during work and builds from the model.
- Scope: In: adding a part found during work, changing a part from approved words and the edit standard, taking a part out on a named approval, the links between parts, the ready check, the structure check, the gap finder and closer, listing what serves an intent, the index through two skills, and, not built yet, each part's approval state, moving the model with the work, one tool that knows the model's shape, and proposing what to try next. Out: the ontology and its change step, the model's first pages, drift from what was built, moving a model to a new version's shape, changes worked out from use, the way past a gate, choosing a slice, and the review where a person answers each piece.

## Expectation
- Scenario: In a clinic's booking app, while Booking is built, the developer finds a Send a reminder feature the model lacks. Its thin page, its thin ICE page and their entries land in the open change, the link to the intent "patients book online" is on both sides, the structure check lists one finding and stops nothing, and the list of what serves that intent now names the feature. In a bank's payments service, the product owner approves merging an old Statements capability into Accounts; the take-out names the product owner, Statements' features and links move to Accounts, and no page or entry still names Statements.
- Done means: Someone other than the one who made the changes reads the index, the pages, the skills' reports and the checks' reports, and finds the clinic's feature with its page, its ICE page, its entries and both thin marks in agreement, its intent link on both sides and the check's finding listed with no stop; and, for the bank, the approver's name in the take-out's report, Statements' features under Accounts, and no page or entry that names Statements.
